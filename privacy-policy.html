HireCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 February 2026

HireCheck ("we", "us") is a Chrome browser extension that analyzes job listings on Naukri.com to detect ghost jobs, scam listings, and toxic workplace signals. This policy describes what data we collect, why, and how we handle it. We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

1. Data We Collect

We do NOT collect: your name, email address, Naukri login credentials, browsing history, or any data from pages other than job listing pages you choose to analyze.
  • Device fingerprint — A randomly generated UUID stored in your browser's local storage. Used solely for rate limiting (5 free analyses per day). This is not linked to your identity.
  • IP address — Logged on each API request for rate limiting and abuse prevention. Retained for 90 days, then deleted.
  • Job listing data — When you click "Analyze", the job title, company name, description, requirements, salary text, and posting date are sent to our server for AI analysis. This data is cached to speed up repeated analyses of the same listing.
  • Feedback — If you click "Actually Real" or "Confirmed Suspicious", we store your feedback (linked to the analysis and your device fingerprint) to improve our detection accuracy.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Analyze job listings using AI models to generate a ghost score and red flags
  • Cache analysis results to reduce redundant API calls (24-hour TTL)
  • Enforce daily rate limits per device to ensure fair usage
  • Improve detection accuracy using aggregated, anonymized feedback

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party APIs to process job listings:

  • Google Gemini API (primary) — Job listing text is sent to Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash model for analysis. See Google AI Terms.
  • OpenAI API (fallback) — If Gemini is unavailable, listing text is sent to OpenAI's GPT-4o-mini model. See OpenAI Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry (error monitoring) — Anonymous error reports may be sent to Sentry for debugging. No personal data is included.

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • API usage logs — Device hash + IP address retained for 90 days
  • Analysis cache — Cached analysis results stored indefinitely (no personal data; only job listing content and scores)
  • Local history — Your last 50 analyses are stored in your browser's local storage only. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to analyze a listing again.
  • Feedback — Stored indefinitely to improve detection models

5. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of data associated with your device fingerprint
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — Request deletion of data associated with your device fingerprint

To exercise these rights, email privacy@hirecheck.in with your device fingerprint (found in the extension's developer console under chrome.storage.local).

6. Data Security

All communication between the extension and our server uses HTTPS. API requests are authenticated using HMAC-SHA256 signatures to prevent tampering. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties beyond the AI processing services listed above.

7. Children

HireCheck is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via a notice in the extension popup. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at privacy@hirecheck.in.
